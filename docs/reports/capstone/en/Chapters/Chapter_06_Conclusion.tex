\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}

\section{Summary of Achievements}

This capstone designed, implemented, and evaluated a coordinated Dual-Agent AI system for Vietnamese retail stores uniting \texttt{Retail-AI-} (customer advisory) and \texttt{ops\_chest} (perishable store operations) through the \texttt{ops-retail-bridge} crosswalk and read-only API layer. Mapped 1-to-1 to the four specific objectives stated in Chapter~1, our achievements are:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Objective 1 -- Customer Advisory Agent (\emph{Zero-Planner Hybrid Routing}):} Designing deterministic-first routing with pre-LLM safety/OWASP gates achieved 47/50 (\textbf{94.0\%}) accuracy on a strictly independent 50-question held-out test set (100\% SKU grounding, 10/10 safety/OWASP) at \textbf{P50 = 74.6\,ms, P95 = 139.0\,ms, P99 = 154.5\,ms} (pre-LLM gates cut P99 by $2.8\times$), achieved \textbf{188--887\,ms} across all 11 queries live on Singapore \texttt{sin1}, scored 100/100 on the Bestseller \& Stock suite backed by synchronized 30-day sales (\code{synced_sales_30d.json}, 269,936 units), delivered 2,205/3,099 zero-defect enriched knowledge records (71.15\%), and trained an SVD-distilled \texttt{MobileNetV3Large-224} TFLite embedder achieving 92.67\% multi-angle Top-1 and 93.8\% Brand@1 / 100\% OOD rejection in Hybrid mode.
    \item \textbf{Objective 2 -- Perishable Operations Agent (\emph{Profit-Gated Router \texttt{B-Gate}}):} Across 50 held-out FreshRetailNet-50K stores, hourly censored-demand recovery reduced stockout-day WAPE from 32.35\% to 20.53\% (bias $-32.35\% \to -0.15\%$); LightGBM~v3 (29 features trained on 14.91\,M rows from 848 disjoint stores) cut evaluation WAPE from 35.06\% (EWMA) to 30.17\% (81.2\% coverage). In 37-day 2-fold CV backtests, \texttt{B-Gate} (pairing LightGBM $\hat{q}_{50}$ with $\sigma_{\text{EWMA}}$ at \code{size_cut=30}) raised median simulated gross profit by \textbf{+4.90\%} over Baseline~A and \textbf{+0.78\%} over EWMA (95\% bootstrap CI $[+0.48\%,\,+1.21\%]$, Wilcoxon $p = 6.39\times 10^{-6}$, winning 76\% of stores; 86.5\% on stores $>30$ SKUs), reduced waste to 4.82\%, cut traffic-driver stockouts from 8.69\% to 3.37\%, and enforced structural HITL governance with zero direct-execution tools.
    \item \textbf{Objective 3 -- Inter-Agent Data Exchange and Coordination:} Built the shared 164-SKU crosswalk (\code{data/bridge/crosswalk.csv}) and fail-closed read-only bridge (\code{backend/services/ops_bridge.py}), verifying end-to-end in a simulated multi-agent environment (\code{tests/test_ops_bridge_e2e.py}) that approved FEFO markdowns propagate immediately, \texttt{PENDING} proposals remain isolated, live stockouts are suppressed from in-stock recommendations, and stale/offline states fail closed safely.
    \item \textbf{Objective 4 -- Controlled Scientific Evaluation:} Enforced strict separation between Dev and independent Test sets, executed controlled ablations (A1--A4, B0--B5), quantified statistical uncertainty via bootstrap CIs, store-size subgroups, and 8-scenario sensitivity analysis, and documented residual safety/denoising limitations transparently.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations and Future Work}

Bounding all conclusions strictly to observed experimental evidence, five research and engineering priorities remain for future work:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Live ERP Integration and 4-Week Physical Store A/B Pilot:} Connect \code{ops_bridge.py} to a real convenience-chain POS/ERP feed and run a 4-week pilot across 3--5 physical stores to verify realized profit, supplier lead-time variability, and manager HITL adoption beyond simulation.
    \item \textbf{Entity-Bound Sub-1,000 Numerical Grounding:} Extend \code{guard.py} to verify all quantities, markdown percentages, and expiry horizons below 1,000 together with strict \emph{(SKU, attribute, value)} entity binding, closing the blind spot identified in Section~5.5.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Accent Acoustic Field Benchmark (100--200 Utterances):} Collect a balanced multi-speaker Vietnamese supermarket speech corpus (Northern, Central, and Southern accents across varied distances and ambient noise) to evaluate ASR generalization statistically and design adaptive SNR-gated denoising avoiding 5\,dB tonal degradation.
    \item \textbf{Expanded Nutrition Labels and Bilingual Alias Coverage:} Extend verified back-of-pack nutrition/calorie coverage across the remaining 55.7\% of SKUs and broaden English--Vietnamese lexical mappings (resolving held-out misses such as \emph{``soy sauce''}).
    \item \textbf{End-to-End Decision-Focused Learning and Local LLM Benchmarking:} Investigate replacing discrete segment gating (\texttt{B-Gate}) with differentiable SPO+ losses~\cite{elmachtoub2022spo} and multi-store rebalancing, and empirically benchmark locally hosted quantized LLMs rather than extrapolating unmeasured latency targets.
\end{enumerate}
